Whether writing sonnets, free verse, hip-hop lyrics, or lyrical prose, gown-ted provides a complete phonological and poetics suite right beside your editor.

![Poetics Suite in gown-ted](/gown-ted/images/hero-editor.png)

## Phonological Breakdown

Every selected word is automatically broken down by its sound components:
- **Syllable Counting:** Accurate syllable count for rhythmic pacing.
- **Rime & Rhyming Ending:** Identifies the nucleus and coda phonemes (e.g. `-ɪspər` for *whisper*).
- **Onset Cluster:** Isolates initial consonant sounds (e.g. `wh-`, `str-`, `bl-`) for alliteration.
- **Consonant-Vowel (CV) Skeleton:** Represents the rhythmic structure (e.g. `CVCCVC`), helping you find words with matching cadence.

---

## Rhyme Discovery & Syllable Filtering

The **Rhythm & Rhyme** panel displays rhyming candidates filtered by syllable count:
- **All Syllables:** Comprehensive list of perfect and near rhymes.
- **1-Syllable Rhymes:** Punchy, monosyllabic line endings.
- **2-Syllable Rhymes:** Feminine rhymes for flowing verse.
- **3+ Syllables:** Polysyllabic and compound rhymes.

Click the **Replace** icon next to any suggestion to substitute it directly into your document, or **Copy** to clipboard.

## Alliterative & Sound-Similar Words

- **Alliterative Onset:** Words sharing identical consonant onsets to construct sonic resonance across poetic lines.
- **Cadence & CV Harmony:** Words sharing syllabic consonant-vowel patterns to create rhythmic internal symmetry.
