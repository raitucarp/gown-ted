Crafted for Deep Lexical Mastery

Everything you need to sculpt words with nuance, precision, and musicality.

🧠

Real-Time Sense Disambiguation

Automatic Lesk contextual analysis identifies the exact WordNet synset matching your sentence context with real-time confidence scores.

🎵

Poetics, Rhyme & Meter

Instant phonological analysis: find exact rhymes, slant rhymes, alliterative onsets, syllable metrics, and Consonant-Vowel (CV) meter patterns.

🌿

Ontology & Semantic Relations

Navigate hypernyms, hyponyms, meronyms, and semantic taxonomy trees. Calculate Wu-Palmer conceptual similarity between any two words.

🔬

Systemic Functional Linguistics

Analyze Theme/Rheme clauses, interpersonal politeness hedging, speech acts, and cohesive lexical ties across your writing.

⚡

Local-First & Blazing Fast

100% offline, zero cloud reliance. Native Go backend powered by Wails v3 starts in milliseconds and consumes minimal RAM.

🎨

Chakra UI v3 Dark Experience

Immersive, customizable multi-pane workspace with distraction-free focus mode, rich text formatting, and tabbed file explorer.

Deep Pragmatic & Speech Act Insights

Unpack the communicative intent behind every word. gown-ted breaks down illocutionary force, performative verbs, interpersonal politeness strategies, and deictic markers in the collapsible bottom drawer.

  • Illocutionary Force: Detect assertive, directive, commissive, and expressive speech acts.
  • Politeness Strategies: Track hedging percentages and mitigation register elements.
  • Theme & Rheme: Examine informational progression from given themes to new rhemes.

Designed for Writers, Poets & Scholars

Whether crafting lyrical poetry, technical essays, or narrative fiction, gown-ted provides the exact vocabulary, synsets, and cadence you need without breaking your creative flow.

  • Distraction-Free Focus Mode: Hide sidebars with Ctrl+Shift+F and immerse yourself in text.
  • Markdown & Plain Text: Work seamlessly with standard .md and .txt files.
  • Open Source & Independent: Fully open source under MIT License, built with Go & React.
